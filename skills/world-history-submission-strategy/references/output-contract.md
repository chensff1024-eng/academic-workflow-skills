# Output contract

Return a strategy with these sections:

1. `Manuscript profile`: topic, period, geography, materials, method, claim,
   conversation, and audience.
2. `Candidate matrix`: venue, journal form, fit reasons, mismatch risks, source
   link, evidence date, and confidence.
3. `Current-fact verification`: author instructions, submission channel,
   formatting, fees, review cycle, indexing, contacts, and AI policy.
4. `Sequence`: first submission, backups, and conditional stretch candidate,
   with reformatting and opportunity-cost notes.
5. `Three revision priorities`: manuscript-specific changes supported by the
   analysis; never invent new sources or findings.
6. `Boundary`: state that no login, upload, email, payment, or final submission
   occurred.

Do not equate ranking with acceptance probability. A candidate with strong
topic overlap can still be unsuitable because of argument, evidence, length,
method, or current editorial priorities.
