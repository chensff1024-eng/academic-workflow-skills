# Evidence policy

Journal advice mixes slow-changing editorial scope with fast-changing
operational facts. Keep them separate.

## Stable enough for a dated discovery snapshot

- disciplinary orientation;
- broad chronological or regional scope;
- whether a venue is general-history, specialist, or theory-oriented;
- recurring topic families visible in official descriptions and contents.

These remain editorial-fit inferences, not guarantees that a manuscript will be
sent for review or accepted.

## Must be verified at the time of use

- current author instructions and submission channel;
- issue frequency, word limits, style, anonymity, and file requirements;
- fees or payments of any kind;
- review cycle and publication schedule;
- indexing or evaluation-list status;
- editorial contacts and special calls;
- research-integrity and generative-AI policies.

Use the journal, publisher, or host institution's official page. Record the URL
and check date. If the official page is unavailable or conflicting, label the
fact unresolved rather than filling it from memory or a journal-agent website.
