# Manual search playbook

## 1. Define the question

Record the object, place, period, mechanism, evidence type, and scholarly
conversation. Separate the question you want to answer from terms likely to
appear in titles and abstracts.

## 2. Build concept groups

For each concept, list modern terms, historical names, abbreviations,
translations, broader categories, narrower terms, and likely spelling variants.
Keep exclusions explicit.

## 3. Run searches manually

Use the CNKI interface and account available to you. Start broad, inspect a
small result sample, revise terms, then use title, keyword, author, journal, and
date filters as needed. Record the query, filters, sort order, date, and result
count in your own search log.

## 4. Export permitted metadata

Export or enter only the fields allowed by your access agreement and
institutional policy. Prefer bibliographic metadata, abstracts, DOI or public
record locators, and your own notes. Do not automate document downloads or
redistribute downloaded material.

## 5. Screen in two passes

- Discovery pass: title, keywords, year, publication, and abstract.
- Evidence pass: inspect the source itself before relying on its detailed
  claims, method, quotations, or page references.

Log exclusions with a short reason so the review remains reproducible.
